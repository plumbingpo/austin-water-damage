A sewage backup is one of the most hazardous situations a property owner can face. Raw sewage contains dangerous bacteria, viruses, parasites and other pathogens, including E. coli, hepatitis, salmonella and giardia, that pose serious health risks to anyone who touches contaminated materials. Across Austin, Austin Restoration Co answers sewage emergencies 24/7, sending trained technicians who arrive with EPA-registered disinfectants and the right protective equipment so your property is decontaminated safely.

The industry classifies sewage as black water, or Category 3, which is the most dangerous and most contaminated type of water damage there is. That rating calls for the strictest protocols: containment barriers go up, crews wear full protective equipment, every porous material the sewage touched is removed, and the surfaces that stay are disinfected thoroughly. Our technicians follow IICRC S500 standards for Category 3 water damage so your property is restored to safe occupancy standards.

The cleanup process involves removing all standing sewage, extracting contaminated water from structural materials, removing and properly disposing of every porous material that contacted sewage (carpet, padding, drywall, insulation), thoroughly disinfecting hard surfaces, applying antimicrobial treatments and completing structural drying to prevent secondary mold growth. Lingering sewage odor is eliminated with thermal fogging and industrial hydroxyl generators, which we run in the affected area.

Warning Signs for Sewage Cleanup

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Sluggish drainage at all fixtures, a sign the main line is blocking up
  • Toilets or drains that gurgle whenever you run water somewhere else
  • Multiple drains in your home backing up at the same time
  • Dark, dirty water or sewage rising out of a tub, toilet or floor drain
  • Foul-smelling moisture showing up on flooring or along the walls
  • Unexplained illness or gastrointestinal symptoms among household members
  • Lower levels or drain openings giving off a heavy sewage odor, sometimes like rotten eggs

Maintenance Tips for Sewage Cleanup

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • A backwater valve at the sewer connection stops the city system from backing up into your home
  • Never pour grease, oil or food waste down your drains, since these cause blockages
  • Hair and other debris are easy to stop with simple strainers on tub, shower and sink drains
  • Have roots removed where trees grow by the sewer, before they get inside the pipe and plug it
  • Toilet paper is the only thing to flush, and that rules out wipes sold as flushable, which still clog
  • Find your sewer cleanout now and check its condition, so it can be reached in an emergency

Why Professional Sewage Cleanup Matters

Sewage cleanup is not something any homeowner should attempt. Handling raw sewage safely takes specialized training, EPA-registered disinfectants that consumers cannot buy, and personal protective equipment, meaning respirators, Tyvek suits and protective boots and gloves. Exposure to sewage pathogens can cause serious illness, including gastrointestinal disease, hepatitis, respiratory infections and skin infections.

Disposal matters as well, since everything the sewage contaminated has to be handled as biohazard waste. Simply placing sewage-contaminated drywall, carpet and insulation in your regular trash is both illegal and hazardous to sanitation workers and the community. A certified restoration company follows proper disposal protocols and works with licensed facilities that accept biohazard waste.
