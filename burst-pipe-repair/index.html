A burst pipe is one of the most common water damage events a property can face, and potentially one of the most destructive. At 4 to 8 gallons a minute, just one burst can send 240 to 480 gallons each hour into your ceilings, walls and flooring. Pipes burst from freezing, old plumbing, too much water pressure, corrosion or a physical hit, and in every case a fast emergency response is what keeps water from ruining flooring, structural materials, building systems and your belongings.

Austin Restoration Co provides 24/7 emergency response for burst pipe water damage throughout Austin and surrounding areas. Technicians on our team assist with finding and closing the water supply, while licensed plumbers we coordinate with handle a proper plumbing repair. With the source under control, our crew goes straight to extraction, using truck-mounted machines and portable extractors to clear standing water from each affected area quickly.

Following extraction, our structural drying process addresses all moisture-affected materials, including drywall, insulation, wood framing, subflooring and finished flooring. Since water runs downhill through ceiling spaces, floor assemblies and wall cavities, a burst pipe frequently affects several rooms on more than one level. Hidden moisture leads to mold and a deteriorating structure, so we use moisture detection equipment and thermal imaging to map the water's path and make sure all affected areas get dried.

Why Burst Pipe Repair Is Not a DIY Job

The damage from a burst pipe tends to reach well past the wet areas in plain view. Water travels through wall cavities, along electrical conduits, through floor assemblies and between ceiling joists, saturating materials in places you may not discover for days or weeks. At that point mold can already be growing out of sight, and the structure may have lost a significant amount of strength. Professional restoration technicians use thermal imaging cameras and moisture meters to trace water migration and identify all affected areas immediately.

The machines we use for extraction and drying are in another class from anything sold for home use. A truck-mounted unit clears water at hundreds of times the speed of a shop vac, while a commercial dehumidifier takes 10 to 20 times more moisture out of the air than a household model. With only 24 to 48 hours before mold starts, you need equipment this fast and this strong.

How to Prevent Burst Pipe Repair Problems

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Never shut the furnace off completely during a winter trip, or pipes inside may freeze
  • Find the main shutoff valve now and practice closing it so you can do it fast
  • Before winter, drain outside spigots and take the hoses off them
  • A plumbing inspection by a licensed pro every few years catches corrosion and wear early
  • Pressure higher than 80 PSI wears on pipes and their fittings, so test yours from time to time
  • Replace old galvanized steel or polybutylene pipes before they fail unexpectedly

When to Call for Burst Pipe Repair in Austin

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • No water at a tap in freezing weather, a sign the line is frozen
  • Water pooling on floors with no apparent source
  • Damp, moldy odors around sinks, tubs or walls that hide pipes
  • Hearing water move through the house while nothing is turned on
  • Tap water that turns brown, yellow or cloudy
  • An unusually high water bill that points to a hidden leak
  • Visible water spraying or dripping from exposed pipes
